Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

At LinkinLabs, accessible via https://linkinlabsgh.pfsgh.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and registered users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected, recorded, and handled by our platform, and how we protect your food analysis data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our platform to coordinate testing services, we collect several types of operational and personal indicators to process requests efficiently:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, professional email address, organization name, telephone contact details, and secure login variables.
  • Sample Metadata: Descriptions, nutritional types, production parameters, batch identification references, and storage constraints necessary for lab assessments.
  • Transactional Logs: Request pipelines, courier dispatch selections, payment status records, and system actions performed within your client portal.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process data under strict regulatory mandates to deliver cohesive logistical services. Your information is used to:

  • Route physical samples safely from your custody directly to verified laboratory testing centers.
  • Generate analytical summaries, progress logs, status milestone alerts, and electronic compliance certificates.
  • Maintain secure operational infrastructure, analyze interface metrics, and troubleshoot server performance bottlenecks.
  • Communicate vital safety indicators or platform status modifications directly to registered users.

3. Data Sharing and Laboratory Disclosure

LinkinLabs functions as a connecting framework. We explicitly share sample criteria and required data parameters exclusively with our trusted laboratory partners (such as the FDA, GSA, KNUST, NOGUCHI, or UHAS) to fulfill your requested testing demands. We do not rent, distribute, sell, or monetize client rosters to external marketing organizations under any circumstances.

4. Security Measures and Retaining Custody

We implement modern end-to-end server firewalls, relational access restriction controls, and encryption layers to safeguard sensitive lab certifications. Account metrics are held within our relational databases for as long as your user profile remains open or as mandated by regional food governance policies.

5. Your Data Access Rights

You retain full operational authority over your platform tracking indices. You have the right to request clear duplicates of your testing summaries, update outdated account particulars, or request the closure and permanent scrubbing of your profile tracking history by logging a formal request to our desk.

6. Revisions to this Privacy Framework

We reserve the right to update this logistical privacy framework dynamically as food metrics standards alter. Any structural changes will be announced explicitly on this webpage with a refreshed revision date indicator.
