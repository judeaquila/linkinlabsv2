Our Standards

Accuracy isn't optional when it comes to the food we eat. Here is how we ensure every result returned to your dashboard is rock-solid and verified.

Network Rule

100% Accredited Labs

We do not own the laboratories, nor do we run random backyard test setups. LinkinLabs connects you exclusively with vetted, fully accredited national and international partner facilities.

Every partner facility in our network maintains strict certifications, meaning your test results carry official weight with health inspectors, retail chains, and international border customs.

Logistics Rule

Unbroken Chain of Custody

A laboratory test is only as good as the sample being tested. If a sample spoils or changes temperature during transit, your results are ruined.

Our tracking workflow mandates clear hand-off checkpoints. Couriers log physical parameters the second they collect your package, protecting your samples from contamination or swap-ups.

Data Rule

Tamper-Proof Records

Once a lab uploads your final report, the metrics are permanent. No one—not even our internal platform administrators—can alter, trim, or adjust numbers on your certificate.

This complete transparency builds deep, unquestionable trust between you, your bulk distributors, and government food safety regulators.
